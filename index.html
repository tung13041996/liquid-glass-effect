Liquid Glass Studio

Uses the same parameters as Figma's Glass effect. Upload your own background, tune the glass, then copy the code from the result box.

Liquid Glass

Drag the glass to move it. You can also drop an image onto the preview. Images stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Glass

Direction the light comes from. The bright 1px rim sits on the edge facing the light, with a slightly dimmer rim on the opposite edge. −45° lights the top-left.
°
Brightness of the light on the glass: the 1px rim and the soft glow inside the curved edge. 0% removes both.
%
How strongly the curved edge bends the image. At 100 the very edge shows the image from a full Depth further inside; the bend fades toward the inner side of the curve.
How far the curved edge reaches inward from the border, in pixels. Higher values make a more domed glass. It is capped at half the shorter side.
Rainbow splitting along the curved edge: red bends a little more and blue a little less than green. Only visible where the glass bends.
Background blur on the glass. The blur radius is about half the Frost value in pixels.
How far the projected light spreads over the glass. It changes the soft glow, not the refraction.

Layer

Tint colour of the glass layer. Pick a colour or type a hex value such as #ffffff.
How strong the tint is. 0% is fully clear; higher values make the colour more solid.
%
Width of the glass in the preview. The static CSS output uses this exact size.
px
Height of the glass in the preview. The static CSS output uses this exact size.
px

Edge

Cuts a torn-paper edge into one side of the glass, like the Figma Glass edge in your design. The glass, its refraction and the tint are all cut along the tear.
How far the tear reaches into the glass, in pixels. 4–10px looks like a fine paper edge.
px
Picks a different random tear shape. The same number always gives the same shape.

How the Figma parameters map

    

      
FigmaWebGL shader

      
        
RefractionThe glass acts as one lens. Within Depth of the edge, each pixel shows the image from further inside, along the normal of a pill-shaped curve. The bend at the edge is Refraction × Depth px and fades inward as t3.5.

        
DepthWidth of the curved edge in pixels, capped at half the shorter side.

        
SplaySpread of the soft light inside the curved edge. It does not change the refraction.

        
DispersionRed is sampled with 10% more bend and blue with 10% less than green at 100. Three texture reads in the same pass.

        
FrostGaussian blur of the source image with radius Frost ÷ 2 px, computed once per image, not per frame.

        
Fill colour + opacityBlended over the refracted image inside the shader.

        
Light angle + %A 1px rim, brightest where the edge faces the light, plus a faint glow inside the curve with an overlay blend.

        
Torn edgemask-image with a generated speckled tear along one side, applied to the glass layer.

      
    

    
    
      
  • The glass refracts a known background: a background-image, <img> or <video> behind it. It cannot bend live page content such as text, so use it for glass over images and video.
    • 
      
  • Works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. One WebGL context is shared by every glass on the page, and each glass is redrawn only when its size, position or image changes.
    • 
      
  • Where WebGL is missing or runs without a GPU (old or blocklisted graphics drivers), the glass falls back to frosted CSS glass with the same fill and highlight.
    • 
      
  • Images from another domain need CORS headers to be used as a texture. Images in your own media library are fine.
    • 
      
  • Corner radius comes from your own CSS border-radius. Put content in a sibling with position: relative; z-index: 1 so it sits above the glass.
    • 
      
  • The formulas were measured against Figma's own render of a real Glass layer, pixel by pixel. Figma does not publish its shader, so results are very close but not pixel-identical. Note that Figma's fill is part of the layer: if your layer uses a black 10% fill, set the fill colour to #000000.
