RefractionfeDisplacementMap scale. 100 bends the edges by 160px.

        
DepthWidth of the curved rim in the displacement map.

        
SplaySoftness of the map's inner edge. Higher values carry the bend further toward the centre.

        
DispersionSeparate red, green and blue displacement passes at slightly different strengths, recombined with screen.

  • Refraction and dispersion render in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers. Safari and Firefox cannot use an SVG filter in backdrop-filter, so they get frosted glass with the same fill and highlight.

  • The responsive version redraws the displacement map when the element resizes. The static version is fixed to the size shown in the preview.

  • Each lens element is GPU-heavy. Keep it to a few per screen and avoid animating their size.
